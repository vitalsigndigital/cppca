Programs · Families

A trusted matrimonial service

CPPCA's Matrimonial Committee helps Pakhtoon families in Ontario find a suitable match for their children within the community. The service is free, confidential and open to members and non-members of Pakhtoon origin.

Contact the committee

Last updated October 13, 2026

Why this service exists

Pakhtoon families often prefer marriages within their own community. Living far from home, with limited community networks in the GTA, finding a suitable partner for a son or daughter can be genuinely hard. CPPCA's board set up the Matrimonial Committee to act as a bridge between families who may be interested in each other.

The committee's role is bridging only. Once two families are introduced, it is entirely up to them to meet and decide how to proceed — the committee takes no part in their discussions. More than 40 families across Canada and the US have been assisted since 2023.

Two ways to share a profile

Option A — the families' WhatsApp group

Families who share a profile on CPPCA's approved form, and agree to it being shared, can join a WhatsApp group run by the committee's admins and browse the other profiles themselves. Profiles do not reveal the candidate's identity. If a family is interested, an admin releases contact details only after both families consent.

Option B — the sub-committee only

If you prefer more privacy, your profile stays with the two-person sub-committee (one woman and one man) and is not posted in the group. It is shared only with a family whose profile matches yours.

Confidentiality

How your information is protected

Under the committee's governing rules, the sub-committee:

  • Keeps profiles to itself and shares them only with families who have also submitted a profile, and only when there is a match
  • Stores profiles in a password-protected spreadsheet that only the two sub-committee members can access
  • Destroys a family's record whenever they ask, and removes it once a marriage is concluded
  • Never discloses information received from community members to anyone who should not have it
  • Hands records securely to a replacement member — or to the CPPCA President — if a member steps down
  • Reports on its work each year at CPPCA's Annual General Meeting

Please don't send profiles, photos or personal details through this website's contact form. Email the committee and a sub-committee member will reply with the approved profile form.
