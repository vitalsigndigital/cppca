Canadian Pakistani Pakhtoon Community Association

A home for Pakhtoon families across Ontario

Since 2005, CPPCA has brought Pakistani Pakhtoon (Pashtun) families in Ontario together — teaching Pashto to the next generation, caring for our elders, standing by families in hard times, and building toward a community centre of our own.

Who we are

One community, two homelands

The Canadian Pakistani Pakhtoon Community Association (CPPCA) is a volunteer-run non-profit for Pakistani Pakhtoon families living in Ontario. Founded in Mississauga in 2005, it keeps Pashto language and Pakhtoon culture alive in Canada and helps members support one another — from a newcomer's first job search to a family's hardest days.

01

Keep our language and culture alive

Weekly online Pashto classes, Pashto mushaira, cultural festivals, Eid gatherings and a calendar of family events across the GTA and Waterloo Region.

02

Stand by one another

An Emergency Support Fund for members' families, help for newcomers, a confidential matrimonial service and programs for seniors, women and youth.

03

Build a home of our own

A reserve fund — already $40,000 strong — toward a permanent Pakhtoon community centre in the Greater Toronto Area.

The next chapter

A Pakhtoon community centre for the GTA

CPPCA's long-term goal is a permanent home for the community in the Greater Toronto Area — a place to gather, learn and celebrate, open to all Canadians. The board's plan for the centre includes:

  • A community hall for social, cultural and sports gatherings
  • Prayer and funeral facilities for the community's religious and cultural needs
  • Guest housing for visitors and community members
  • A CPPCA office for newcomer support, Pashto education and cultural programs

Questions

Common questions

Who can join CPPCA?

CPPCA was founded for Pakistani Pakhtoon families and individuals living in Canada, and they make up its membership. Applications are reviewed by CPPCA's Executive Council under the association's by-laws. Many CPPCA events — BBQs, festivals and gatherings — welcome families who are not yet members. How to join.

How much is membership?

$60 a year for an individual or $120 a year for a family (spouses and unmarried children living in Canada). You can also pay $5 or $10 a month by automatic bank withdrawal. Part of every fee goes to the Emergency Support Fund and the Community Centre Reserve Fund.

Is CPPCA a registered charity?

Not yet. CPPCA is a volunteer-run non-profit corporation in Ontario. Its application for registered-charity status has been filed with the Canada Revenue Agency and is pending, so CPPCA cannot issue official donation receipts for income-tax purposes.

Do I need to speak Pashto?

No. Many members' children are growing up with English first — which is exactly why CPPCA runs weekly online Pashto classes.

Where are CPPCA events held?

Across the GTA and beyond. Recent events took place in Mississauga, Toronto (Thorncliffe Park), Brampton, Milton and Cambridge, plus trips to Muskoka and Niagara.

How is CPPCA run?

By a 15-member volunteer Board of Directors elected by members for two-year terms, supported by committees for finance, events, media, records, newcomers, youth, women and more. Elections are run by an independent Election & Disciplinary Commission. More about CPPCA.
